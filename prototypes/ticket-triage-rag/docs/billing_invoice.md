# Billing & Invoices

Invoices are generated on the 1st of each month and emailed to the account owner. You can view past invoices under Settings > Billing > Invoice History. Charges are billed in the currency selected at signup and cannot be changed mid-cycle. Refunds for accidental duplicate charges are processed within 5-7 business days back to the original payment method. If a card payment fails, we retry 3 times over 7 days before downgrading the account to the free tier; you'll get an email warning before any downgrade.
