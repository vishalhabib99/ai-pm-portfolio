# Password Reset

To reset your password: go to the login page and click "Forgot password". Enter the email on your account and we'll send a reset link valid for 30 minutes. If you don't receive the email within 5 minutes, check your spam folder. Accounts with SSO enabled (Google/Microsoft login) cannot reset a password here — sign in through your SSO provider instead. If you're locked out after 5 failed attempts, the lock clears automatically after 15 minutes.
